About the Portuguese Language

  • Speakers: 260 million native and second-language speakers worldwide
  • Script: Latin alphabet
  • Main regions: Brazil, Portugal, Angola, Mozambique and worldwide
  • Brazilian vs European. Brazilian Portuguese (PT-BR) and European Portuguese (PT-PT) differ in accent, vocabulary and some grammar. This translator defaults to Brazilian Portuguese, which has the most speakers.
  • Nasal vowels. Portuguese has nasal vowels (ã, ão, em, im) that do not exist in English. The audio playback will pronounce them correctly — listen and repeat for accurate pronunciation.
  • Gender on nouns. Like Spanish and French, Portuguese nouns are masculine or feminine. 'Obrigado' (thank you) is used by men; 'Obrigada' by women — both are included in the phrasebook above.
