What to know about Kannada translation

Kannada is spoken by about 60 million speakers. It uses Kannada script (ಕನ್ನಡ ಲಿಪಿ) and is spoken primarily in Karnataka and Kannada-speaking communities worldwide.

  • Classical language. Kannada has been recognised as a Classical Language of India with a literary history of over 2,500 years.
  • Script similarity. The Kannada script looks similar to Telugu script but they are distinct. If text looks right but sounds wrong, confirm the target language is set to Kannada (ಕನ್ನಡ).
  • Tech vocabulary. Bengaluru's tech industry means Kannada speakers use a lot of English loanwords — software, laptop, internet — which translations keep as-is.
