Before you write: quick fixes

  • The microphone does nothing. Allow microphone access when the browser asks. If you blocked it earlier, click the lock icon next to the address bar, set Microphone to Allow, and reload. Voice input works best in Chrome or Edge.
  • "Translation failed". The translation service is briefly unavailable or your connection dropped. Wait a few seconds and press Translate again; very long text is easier to translate a paragraph at a time.
  • The video call will not connect. Both people need the same room code, a normal browser tab (not an in-app browser such as Instagram's), and camera and microphone allowed. Some office and school networks block peer-to-peer calls.
  • No sound when reading aloud. Check the phone is not on silent. Some languages have no built-in voice on some devices; installing that language's voice in your phone's text-to-speech settings fixes it.

What to include

For a bug, tell us the page, your device and browser (for example "Android, Chrome"), the two languages and what you expected to happen. For a language request, name the language and the region you need it for. Please do not send passwords, ID numbers or other private details.
