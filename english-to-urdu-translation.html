About the Urdu Language

  • Speakers: 230 million native and second-language speakers worldwide
  • Script: Nastaliq script (right to left)
  • Main regions: Pakistan, India and Urdu-speaking communities worldwide
  • Right to left, Nastaliq style. Urdu uses a flowing calligraphic script called Nastaliq, written right to left. It is visually distinct from Arabic even though it shares the same alphabet base.
  • Shared spoken language with Hindi. Spoken Urdu and Hindi are largely mutually intelligible. The difference appears in writing (Urdu: Nastaliq; Hindi: Devanagari) and formal vocabulary.
  • Poetic tradition. Urdu has a rich literary tradition in poetry (shayari). Many English words have entered Urdu via British colonial history and are used naturally alongside Persian and Arabic loanwords.
