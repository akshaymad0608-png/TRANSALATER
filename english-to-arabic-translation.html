About the Arabic Language

  • Speakers: 400 million native and second-language speakers worldwide
  • Script: Arabic script (right to left)
  • Main regions: Middle East, North Africa, and Muslim communities worldwide
  • Right to left. Arabic is written right to left. The translator outputs proper RTL text; copy it into any app and it will display correctly.
  • Modern Standard vs dialects. Modern Standard Arabic (فصحى) is used in formal writing and media across all Arab countries. Spoken dialects vary widely between Egypt, Gulf, Levant and Maghreb regions.
  • Root system. Arabic words are built from 3-letter roots. One root can generate dozens of related words, making it one of the most systematic languages in the world.
