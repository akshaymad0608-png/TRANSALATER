Frequently asked questions

How do I translate English to Hindi for free?

Open Rosetta, keep English as the source and Hindi as the target, then type or speak. The Hindi translation appears instantly — free, with no sign-up.

Can I hear the Hindi pronunciation?

Yes. Tap the speaker button and Rosetta reads the Hindi translation aloud so you can hear how it sounds.

Can I translate Hindi back to English?

Yes — tap the swap button to reverse the direction and translate Hindi to English.

Does it work by voice?

Yes. Tap the microphone and speak your English; Rosetta transcribes and translates it to Hindi. Chrome or Edge work best for voice.

Is it private?

Yes — Rosetta runs in your browser and does not store your translations on a server.
