Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 September 2026

Overview

Rosetta is a free browser-based language translator available at languagetransalator.com, built and maintained by Akshay Mahajan. This policy explains what information is collected when you use Rosetta and how it is used.

Your translations are private

All text and voice translation happens in your browser. Voice and conversation sessions run entirely on your device. Video-call translation is peer-to-peer (no server stores the audio or video). Rosetta does not collect or store any text you translate.

Information collected

  • Analytics data. This site uses Google Analytics to understand how visitors find and use Rosetta. It records page views, approximate location (derived from IP address), device type, and referring source. It does not record the content of your translations.
  • Advertising. This site carries Google AdSense ads. Google may use cookies to serve personalised ads based on your previous browsing activity. You can opt out via Google's ad settings.

Cookies

Google Analytics and Google AdSense set cookies. These are standard third-party cookies used for analytics and ad personalisation. No first-party cookies are set by this website itself (browser storage is used only to remember your language preferences locally on your device).

Third-party services

  • Google Analytics — usage statistics
  • Google AdSense — advertising
  • Google Translate API — translation engine (text is sent to Google's servers to perform translation)
  • MyMemory API — fallback translation engine

Each third-party service has its own privacy policy. Google's policy is at policies.google.com/privacy.

Your rights

You can block analytics and ads using any standard browser extension. If you have questions about data collected via Google services, see Google's privacy policy.
