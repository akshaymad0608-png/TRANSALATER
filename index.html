Language is not a barrier

Rosetta is a free, private translator that runs entirely in your browser. Translate typed or spoken text across 40+ languages, hold a two-way live conversation on a single phone, or make a peer-to-peer video call with real-time translated subtitles — with no sign-up, no API keys and nothing to install.

Text & voice translation

Type a sentence or tap the microphone and speak — Rosetta detects the language, translates instantly, and can read the result aloud. Copy the translation or swap the two languages with one tap.

Live conversation mode

Two people, one phone. Each person picks their language, taps their button and speaks; Rosetta translates and speaks the reply aloud in the other person's language.

Video call with live subtitles

Start a call, share the room code, and each person sees what the other says as real-time translated subtitles. Calls are peer-to-peer, so your conversation stays private.

Talk to anyone, face to face

Ordering at a market, asking a doctor a question, meeting a new colleague: put one phone between you and take turns. Each person taps their own button and speaks; Rosetta writes it down, translates it and reads it aloud in the other person's language.

Every line stays on screen with the original underneath, so either of you can check what was said. Download the transcript when you are done.

Built for every situation

Ask for directions

Open the Quick Phrase Kit for ready-made travel, food, medical and emergency phrases, then tap one to translate it into the local language.

Show, don't shout

On a noisy street, tap Show to person and the translation fills the screen in large type, so the other person can simply read it.

Hear how it sounds

Press the speaker under any translation to play it aloud, so you can let your phone do the talking at a counter or in a taxi.

Calls with live subtitles

Start a video call, share the room code, and both of you read the other's words as translated captions while you talk.

Reply to a message fast

Paste a message or email, read it in your language, write your answer and translate it back before you send.

Meet in person

Conversation mode keeps a two-sided transcript of a face-to-face meeting that you can download afterwards for your notes.

Learn the pronunciation

For scripts such as Hindi, Arabic or Japanese, Rosetta shows a romanised "say it" line under the translation, and the speaker reads it aloud.

Practise speaking

Speak a sentence into the mic in the language you are learning and check whether Rosetta understood what you meant.

Compare both ways

Use the swap button to translate your sentence back again, a quick way to spot words that did not carry the meaning you wanted.

Frequently asked questions

Is Rosetta free?

Yes — completely free, with no sign-up and no API keys, and no limit on how much you translate.

Is my translation private?

Yes. Rosetta runs in your browser and video calls are peer-to-peer, so your conversations are not stored on any server.

How many languages does it support?

40+ world and regional languages, including all the major Indian languages plus Spanish, French, German, Arabic, Chinese, Japanese and Korean.

Can I translate a live conversation on one phone?

Yes — Conversation mode lets two people talk on a single device, translating and reading each reply aloud in the other language.

Can I translate a video call?

Yes. Start a call, share the room code, and each person sees the other's speech as live translated subtitles. Chrome or Edge work best.

Do I need to install an app?

No — Rosetta works in any modern web browser on phone or computer, with nothing to install.
