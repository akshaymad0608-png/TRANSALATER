Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about how Rosetta works.

Is Rosetta free?

Yes — completely free, with no sign-up, no account and no limits. It runs entirely in your browser.

How many languages are supported?

More than 130 world and regional languages, including Hindi, Gujarati, Marathi, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali, Punjabi, Kannada, Malayalam, Odia, Assamese, Konkani, Sanskrit and many more.

Can I translate my voice?

Yes. Tap the microphone and speak; your speech is converted to text, translated, and can be read aloud. Voice input works best in Chrome and Edge.

How does video-call translation work?

Two people connect using a shared room code. Each person's speech is transcribed and translated on their own device, then sent to the other as live subtitles — optionally read aloud too.

Do I need to install anything?

No. It works in any modern browser. You can optionally tap “Add to Home Screen” to use it like an installed app.

Why doesn't voice work for some languages?

Text translation works for all 130+ languages, but speech input and read-aloud depend on the voices your specific browser and device support, so a few languages may be text-only.
